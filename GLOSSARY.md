# Show Tracker

A personal, client-side web application for tracking movies and TV shows, managing watch progress, and curating a watchlist.

## Language

**Title**:
A movie or TV show tracked in the application.
_Avoid_: Media item, entry, show (when referring generally to both movies and TV)

**TV Show**:
An episodic series structured into seasons and episodes.
_Avoid_: Series, program, broadcast

**Movie**:
A standalone feature-length film with a single watched state.
_Avoid_: Film, picture, cinema

**Watchlist**:
The collection of titles marked with the status Plan to Watch.
_Avoid_: Queue, backlog, saved list, bookmarks

**Watch Status**:
The current viewing lifecycle state of a title: Plan to Watch, Watching, Completed, or Dropped.
_Avoid_: State, viewing progress, category

**Episode Progress**:
The record of individual seasons and episodes marked as watched for a TV show.
_Avoid_: Episode tracker, watch history

**Quick Increment**:
A one-click action that advances the user's progress by marking the next sequential episode as watched.
_Avoid_: Plus one button, next episode shortcut

**Rating**:
A 1–10 numerical score assigned by the user to evaluate a title.
_Avoid_: Score, review, stars
